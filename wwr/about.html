About Rafting Travel

Providing safe and thrilling adventures for over 5 years.

Our History

Rafting Travel was founded in 2020 by a group of outdoor enthusiasts who wanted to share their passion for the river with the world. What started as a small operation with two rafts has grown into the premier guide service in the region.


We believe that nature has the power to heal and inspire. Our mission is to provide safe, exciting, and memorable experiences for adventurers of all skill levels.
