Rafting Travel: Make Memories!

Our goal is to provide you with the best experience possible. We have been providing unforgettable experiences for over 30 years. We are a family owned business dedicated to your adventure.

History

Rafting Travel started floating the river in 1995 when a group of friends turned their passion into a business. Our commitment has always been simple: share the thrill of whitewater rafting while preserving the beauty of nature.

From humble beginnings offering local trips, we have grown into a leading provider of comprehensive rafting expeditions worldwide. We believe in sustainable tourism and creating memories that last a lifetime.
